## Hi there! 👋

I'm **Xinan "William" Yan** — an **HCI & XR researcher and systems builder** working on **next-generation spatial interaction**, **gaze & freehand input**, and **human-computer interaction modeling**.

I build research prototypes and developer-facing systems for:
- 👁️ **Gaze & Eye-Tracking Interaction** (task-aligned correction, latency & stability optimization)
- ✋ **Freehand & Spatial Manipulation** (3D steering, polyhedron face selection, multimodal input)
- ⌨️ **Text Entry & Wearable Interaction** (on-body tap typing, spatial UI)
- 📐 **HCI Performance Modeling** (uncertainty modeling, automated evaluation pipelines)

Currently affiliated with **XJTLU**, and **HKUST(GZ)**.
